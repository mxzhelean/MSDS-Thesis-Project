\section{Corpus Overview}
Descriptive statistics of the 300-output corpus detail token distributions, vocabulary richness, and length profiles across model endpoints and task categories.

\section{Task 1 Results: Explanatory Variety Analysis (RQ1/H1)}
Evaluating semantic variance patterns across 50 iterations per model confirms cross-model structural convergence around core mathematical facts. Output variance manifests as constructive pedagogical analogies rather than factual drift, supporting H1.

\section{Task 2 Results: Homogenization Analysis (RQ2/H2)}
Analysis of cross-iteration cosine distance and cross-architecture vector space overlap reveals significant semantic collapse. BERTopic clustering shows heavy concentration around standardized Western academic tropes with reduced coverage of non-dominant perspectives, supporting H2.

\section{Cross-Task Comparison}
Side-by-side comparison of variance profiles between Task 1 and Task 2 validates that output homogenization is strongly task-dependent, confirming the operational model of \cite{Jain2025}.

Summary table and statistical synthesis highlighting contrast between objective explanatory flexibility and subjective epistemic collapse.
